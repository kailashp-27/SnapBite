Model hashes are pinned in ml/mixed-models.json and checked during every build.
The weights are redistributed without modification. SnapBite supplies its own
grid prompting, mask filtering, food-family classifier and portion heuristics.
These models do not measure food weight and were not trained for chapati counts.
