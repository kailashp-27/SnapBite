SnapBite on-device food recognition

Contains a MobileNetV2 backbone and Food-101 classifier derived from:
https://huggingface.co/AlexKoff88/mobilenet_v2_food101
Revision: 7d05d3878ee7f2aad7ad159018a8e5fdb4953d41
Upstream model license: Apache-2.0; see LICENSE-base.txt.

The trained checkpoint was reconstructed into ONNX with evaluation BatchNorm
folding. A regional linear head was trained on the CC0-1.0 dataset:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/bharat-raghunathan/indian-foods-dataset
Revision: ef509a8b4bfd46b76530ad329f81586faf904aad

Weights were compressed with per-channel INT8 weight quantization; activations
remain float32. The original upstream ONNX artifact failed verification and
is not the deployed model. Training and benchmark details: ml/README.md.
